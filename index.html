1. How to Input Your Videos, Photos & Text

  • Click Owner Studio in the footer (or press Shift + E anytime) to activate editing tools.
  • Change Hero Nature Video & Email: Click Hero Video & Brand in the top studio bar to upload your own MP4 nature video, paste a direct video link, and set your personal business email address.
  • Add or Edit Clothing Pieces: Click + Add Garment (or click the pencil icon on any existing item card) to upload clothing photos, attach optional runway/lookbook videos, set sizes, pricing, and descriptions.

2. Hosting Large Videos & Connecting a Custom Domain

  • Permanent Public Media URLs: For fast global streaming, upload your MP4 nature videos and lookbook clips to a free host like Cloudinary or Bunny.net and paste the direct .mp4 URL into Owner Studio before exporting your live index.html.
  • Custom Domain Name: After dropping your site onto Netlify or Vercel, click Domain Settings in their dashboard to connect www.for3stry.com with automatic SSL security.

3. Customer Orders & Direct Email Communication

  • Direct Email Orders & Inquiries: Every message from the Concierge modal and every checkout summary routes directly to your configured owner email address.
  • Instant Card Payments: Create free Stripe Payment Links for your garments when you are ready for automated credit card and Apple Pay payouts.
